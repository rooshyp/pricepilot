# Roadmap

Roadmap items describe direction, not shipped functionality.

## Current — browser MVP

- Product portfolio dashboard with economic KPIs, trends, and derived opportunities
- Product-level history, unit economics, elasticity, and evidence context
- Price Frontier with revenue and contribution-profit optima
- Editable Scenario Lab and elasticity sensitivity analysis
- Deterministic decision brief and pricing experiment proposal
- Synthetic sample records with varied product behavior
- Client-side CSV mapping, preview, validation, and partial valid-row import
- Automated model, import, type, lint, and production-build checks

## Next — strengthen evidence and workflow

- Add promotion and seasonality controls to elasticity estimation
- Detect likely stockouts and influential observations before fitting demand response
- Add experiment sizing inputs and result ingestion
- Save named local scenarios with explicit retention controls
- Export a calculation trace and decision brief with model assumptions attached

## Later — operational decision system

- Persistent, access-controlled workspaces and review history
- Channel, region, and customer-segment demand models
- Pluggable causal and hierarchical models with backtesting
- Governed data connectors and scheduled refreshes
- Approval workflows and monitored pricing experiments

Price execution remains outside scope until recommendations, approvals, and rollback controls can be audited end to end.
